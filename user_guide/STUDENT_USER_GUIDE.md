# Student User Guide: Flight Booking System

Welcome to the Flight Booking System. This guide will walk you through the process of searching for and booking your next flight.

---

## 1. Accessing the System
To get started, navigate to the system URL provided by your administrator. You will be greeted by the secure login screen.

### Step 1: Login
- Enter your **Student ID** and **Password**.
- Click the **Login** button to access your dashboard.

---

## 2. Searching for Flights
Once logged in, you can search for available flights across the Philippines and international destinations.

### Step 2: Search
- Select your **Origin** (where you are flying from).
- Select your **Destination** (where you want to go).
- Choose your **Departure Date**.
- Click **Search Flights**.

### Step 3: Select a Flight
- A list of available flights will appear with prices and schedules.
- Compare the options and click **Book Now** on your preferred flight.

![Booking Dashboard Preview](C:\Users\Crissaunt\.gemini\antigravity\brain\6ee69149-0d83-4608-bac6-561bfa543c00\booking_dashboard_preview_1778783789337.png)

---

## 3. Selecting Your Seat
After choosing a flight, you can pick your favorite seat on the aircraft.

### Step 4: Seat Selection
- Use the interactive seat map to find an available seat (usually shown in green).
- Different classes (Economy, Business) may have different colors.
- Once selected, click **Proceed to Payment**.

![Seat Selection Preview](C:\Users\Crissaunt\.gemini\antigravity\brain\6ee69149-0d83-4608-bac6-561bfa543c00\seat_selection_preview_1778783804619.png)

---

## 4. Payment and Confirmation
The final step is to secure your booking.

### Step 5: Finalize Booking
- Review your flight details and total price.
- Enter your payment details securely.
- Click **Confirm Booking**.
- You will receive an instant confirmation and your itinerary will be saved to your profile.
