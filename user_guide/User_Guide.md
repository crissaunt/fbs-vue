43# Flight Reservation System - User Guide

This guide provides instructions for students on how to use the Flight Reservation System, including the student dashboard, booking process, and online check-in.

## Table of Contents
1. [Student Dashboard](#1-student-dashboard)
2. [Practice Booking](#2-practice-booking)
3. [Online Check-in](#3-online-check-in)

---

## 1. Student Dashboard
The Student Dashboard is your central hub for tracking progress and accessing training simulations.

![Student Dashboard](student_dashboard.png)

**Description:**
* **Academic Achievement:** View your average grade, activities completed, and total points.
* **Score Evolution:** Track your performance over the last 10 tasks.
* **Upcoming Schedule:** Stay informed about your training sessions.
* **Access Portal:** Click the "Access Portal" button at the bottom to enter the simulation environment.

---

## 2. Practice Booking
The Practice Booking module allows you to simulate the flight search and reservation process.

![Practice Booking](booking_page.png)

**Description:**
* **Search Flights:** Select your trip type (One Way, Round Trip, or Multi City), origin, destination, and departure date.
* **Passenger Selection:** Specify the number of passengers for the booking.
* **Search:** Click "SEARCH FLIGHTS" to view available simulated flights.
* **Practice Mode:** The "PRACTICE MODE ACTIVE" indicator confirms you are in a simulation environment.

---

## 3. Online Check-in
The Online Check-in simulation guides you through the self-service check-in protocol.

![Online Check-in](online_checkin.png)

**Description:**
* **Access Protocol:** Enter the PNR (Passenger Name Record) or Reference Number and the Family Name.
* **Protocol Steps:** Follow the 6-step process: Details -> Safety -> Audit -> Link -> Pass -> Clear.
* **Self-Service Portal:** This module mimics the real-world passenger check-in experience at an airport kiosk or website.
